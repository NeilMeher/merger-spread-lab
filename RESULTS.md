All three feasible Spirit post-event values use the $4 assumed recovery. A recovery of $8 or $12 plus pending modeled payments produces a payoff above the $7.92 observed close, even if the completion weight is zero. Those six infeasible scenario cells keep their raw negative weights in the output. Comparing extrema across different feasible cells can obscure that change in support.

The baseline subtracts the change in the S&P 500 over the same trading sessions. Case-specific news, sector exposure and differences in risk remain. UK developments overlapped the Activision ruling. Prior iRobot trading reflected approval speculation. Initial unaffected prices may be stale by the milestone or contaminated by pre-announcement information. These cases were chosen for public price and event evidence, with no sampling inference. In Mitchell and Pulvino’s larger sample, risk-arbitrage exposure differed between falling and flat or rising markets. The simple index subtraction here is descriptive. [Mitchell and Pulvino](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00401)
