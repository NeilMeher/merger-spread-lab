# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18 after the coverage pilot and before evaluating the pricing model. Use three convenience cases for US-listed U.S. common shares in signed all-cash deals, each with a public agency or court milestone and verifiable closes on the preceding and affected sessions. Use the July 2023 Microsoft–Activision injunction denial, the November 2023 Amazon–iRobot statement of objections and the January 2024 JetBlue–Spirit injunction. Keep completed and failed deals. This case series describes these observations and has no training split, representative sampling, or causal identification. Other milestones without a price audit remain in the event ledger. Overlapping news is flagged and retained as part of the descriptive event window.

Primary metric is the change in the feasible range of pricing-implied completion weights, with identical scenarios across the two sessions. Baseline comparison is simple close-to-close target return less S&P 500 price return. Next-session alignment applies at or after the listed ET session close, and premarket events map to the same session. The event ledger distinguishes a decision date from public dissemination. NYSE and Nasdaq holidays and early closes are recorded for these windows in the input file.

Use an annual effective discount rate of 5%, 30 days to break, and 30, 90, or 180 days to close. Break-price scenarios are stress assumptions rather than a mechanically extrapolated unaffected price. Use $4, $8, $12 for SAVE, $15, $22.50, $30 for IRBT and $55, $67.50, $80 for ATVI. Prices outside state payoff bounds appear as infeasible, with raw implied weights retained in the grid. Discount future Spirit payments of $0.10 monthly to each scenario's horizon (first Jan 31, then 30-day approximation); deduct previous payments from closing consideration using original cap. No other incremental payment enters the studied event windows. Recovery values represent total equity per share after any corporation-level termination fees. Treat calculations at milestones before termination as conditional on transaction outcomes still available.
